# Workout Coach Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

## Purpose and operator

Workout Coach is a personal fitness integration operated by the owner of this GitHub account for the owner's own use. It is intended to help review training, sleep, and recovery. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

## Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the integration may retrieve WHOOP sleep records, recovery scores, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, daily strain, workout summaries, and basic body measurements such as height, weight, and maximum heart rate. Only permissions needed for these functions are requested. This policy does not authorize access to other people's accounts.

## Use and storage

Data is used to review personal fitness trends and inform workout planning. Retrieved records and derived summaries may be stored on the owner's computer and in private project storage selected by the owner. OAuth credentials and access or refresh tokens must be stored separately from public policy files and must not be committed to public repositories.

## Third-party services

The owner may provide selected records or summaries to ChatGPT/OpenAI for analysis and coaching. That information is handled under the applicable OpenAI terms, privacy policy, and account data controls. WHOOP handles source data under its own policies. If Postman is used to authorize or test API access, its applicable terms and privacy practices also apply. GitHub hosts this public policy; health records and credentials are not intended to be published here.

The integration does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or intentionally publish health information.

## Retention, access revocation, and deletion

Downloaded data is retained while useful for the owner's personal fitness tracking, until the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the integration's authorization through WHOOP's available account controls or revocation mechanism. Revocation stops future authorized retrieval but does not delete existing downloaded copies. Local files, project copies, and information shared with other services must be deleted separately using each location's controls; those services may have their own retention rules.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the integration's data handling changes. Questions about the integration may be directed to the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not post health information, credentials, or tokens in public issues or comments.
